200ms

Usage:

- Hover ساده

---

Duration:

400ms

Usage:

- حرکت Component ها

Duration:

800ms - 1200ms

Usage:

- باز شدن کارت‌ها

# Interaction Principles


All interactive elements should:

- Have visible hover state
- Have smooth transitions
- Maintain premium feeling
- Avoid sudden movements


KBG interaction style:

Slow
Cinematic
Controlled
Immersive
